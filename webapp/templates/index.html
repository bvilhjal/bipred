Advanced assumptions and controls

At least {{ min_burn_in }} burn-in and {{ min_num_iter }} retained iterations are required. This floor rejects obviously non-diagnostic schedules; it does not establish convergence.

Assumption: zero means no correlated sampling error. Supply an externally justified value when samples overlap; the fitted-panel moment intercept is not substituted automatically because it may also contain confounding.

Mandatory LD-consistency screen: every analysis applies the fixed-parameter, DENTIST-inspired screen to each trait on its own usable principal LD panel before intersection. This is not the complete published DENTIST workflow or a calibrated conservatism dial. Removed-variant counts are reported.
