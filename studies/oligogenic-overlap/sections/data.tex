\section*{Full results}

The figures above are a reading of one table, not a substitute for it.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\texttt{results/estimates.csv} & one row per pair: \texttt{m}, LDSC and bipred
  \texttt{rg}, \texttt{h2}, \\
  & the overlap parameters, cancellation ratios, divergence flag \\
\texttt{results/provenance.json} & package versions, platform, clean source
  revisions \\
\texttt{../\_lib/traits.toml} & every trait: file, column layout, $N$,
  ancestry, known quirks \\
\texttt{../\_lib/datasets.md} & sources tried and rejected, with symptoms \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Thirteen rows are recorded: the eleven fitted pairs above, and two fits of
bilirubin $\times$ gallstones that were rejected on data grounds rather than on
their results. The gallstones release carries minor allele frequency under an
effect-allele-frequency header, and the refit diverged (cancellation $14.29$).
Both rows are kept in the CSV so the rejection is auditable;
\texttt{../\_lib/datasets.md} records how it was diagnosed.

Summary statistics are not committed --- roughly 9\,GB across the thirteen
traits. Nothing in this report requires them: \texttt{figures.py} reads only
\texttt{results/estimates.csv}.
