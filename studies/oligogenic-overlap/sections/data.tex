\section*{Full results}

The figures above are a reading of one table, not a substitute for it.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\texttt{results/estimates.csv} & one row per pair: \texttt{m}, LDSC and bipred
  \texttt{rg}, \texttt{h2}, \\
  & the overlap parameters, cancellation ratios, divergence flag \\
\texttt{results/provenance.json} & package versions, platform, clean source
  revisions \\
\texttt{../\_lib/traits.toml} & every trait: file, column layout, $N$,
  ancestry, known quirks \\
\texttt{../\_lib/datasets.md} & sources tried and rejected, with symptoms \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Twenty-four rows are recorded, under two values of \texttt{fit\_chi2\_cap}:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llr@{}}
\toprule
\texttt{fit\_chi2\_cap} & \texttt{status} & rows \\
\midrule
\texttt{uncapped} & \texttt{ok} --- the study's estimates & 10 \\
\texttt{uncapped} & \texttt{diverged} --- GGT $\times$ bilirubin & 1 \\
\texttt{capped}   & \texttt{ok} --- the superseded comparison & 11 \\
\texttt{capped}   & \texttt{superseded}, \texttt{rejected} & 2 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\texttt{uncapped} means the chi-square cap was applied to the LD Score
regression, which needs it, and not to the joint fit. Those are the rows every
figure and number in this report is drawn from. The \texttt{capped} rows are
kept because the difference between them is a result in its own right.

The two \texttt{capped} rows that are neither \texttt{ok} nor superseded are
fits of bilirubin $\times$ gallstones, rejected on data grounds rather than on
their results: the gallstones release carries minor allele frequency under an
effect-allele-frequency header, and the refit diverged. They are kept so the
rejection is auditable; \texttt{../\_lib/datasets.md} records how it was
diagnosed.

Summary statistics are not committed --- roughly 9\,GB across the thirteen
traits. Nothing in this report requires them: \texttt{figures.py} reads only
\texttt{results/estimates.csv}.
