\subsection*{This analysis}

Three cleaning stages are fitted, and their contrast is the result:

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.3em}
\item \textbf{harmonised} --- rsID match to the LD reference with allele
  matching and sign flipping only. 924{,}254 variants.
\item \textbf{per-variant QC} --- adds MAF $\geq 0.01$, imputation
  INFO $\geq 0.9$, $\chi^2 \leq 80$, and allele-frequency discordance against
  the reference $\leq 0.20$. 887{,}361 variants.
\item \textbf{+ LD-consistency screen} --- adds the screen of the previous
  section at four rounds. 845{,}623 variants (845{,}423 on the second run).
\end{enumerate}

Each stage is a full joint fit at 200 burn-in and 300 retained sweeps, with
\texttt{cross\_corr} taken from that stage's own cross-trait LDSC intercept.
Long-range LD regions and the MHC are retained throughout.

The generating script is \texttt{benchmarks/real\_ldl\_cad.py}, which stays in
\texttt{benchmarks/} because its other role --- does a real-shaped GWAS still
produce an admissible fit? --- is a regression canary that should be regenerated
freely. What belongs here is the estimate and its reading. The two roles want
opposite handling, which is why they live in different places.

Inputs are not committed; \texttt{benchmarks/README.md} Table 4 records how to
acquire them, and \texttt{../\_lib/traits.toml} registers their column layouts.
